```release-note:bug
agent: Blocking queries whose `wait` exceeds `http_config.write_timeout` are no longer reset with `EOF` or an HTTP/2 `INTERNAL_ERROR` stream error. The write deadline for blocking-query requests is now based on the requested `wait` (capped at `max_query_time`) plus jitter and `rpc_hold_timeout`, and is never shorter than `write_timeout`. Non-blocking requests still use `write_timeout`.
```
