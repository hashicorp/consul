```release-note:bug
proxycfg: fixed a bug where a transient "ACL not found" error for a valid token (e.g.
from a Raft follower momentarily behind on replication) would immediately and
permanently tear down a proxy's Connect/xDS configuration. A brief streak of
consecutive occurrences is now tolerated, in both the agent-cache-backed and
server-local materialized-view-backed data source paths, before a proxy's watches are
torn down.
```
